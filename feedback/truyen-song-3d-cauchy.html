Bai toan Cauchy — Phuong trinh truyen song 3D co nguon

Giai $u_{tt} - \Delta u = f$ tren $\mathbb{R}^3$, du lieu ban dau $u(\cdot,0)=0$, $u_t(\cdot,0)=h$, nguon $f = \chi_{[0,1]}(t)\,\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$. Tat ca khai niem trong bai la moi.

1. Phan loai 2. Khai niem 3. Phuong phap 4. Dang bai lan can 5. Ly thuyet
1

Phan loai bai toan

Bac
Bac hai — co $u_{tt}$ va $\Delta u$
Loai
Hyperbolic — symbol $-\tau^2+|\xi|^2$ doi dau
Tuyen tinh?
Co, khong thuan nhat (co nguon $f$ doc lap voi $u$)
Bai toan
IVP (Cauchy) tren $\mathbb{R}^3$, khong co bien khong gian
So chieu
$n=3$ (le) → nguyen ly Huygens manh hoat dong
Toc do song
$c = 1$
Nho So chieu $n=3$ la so le. Day la dieu kien can thiet de nguyen ly Huygens manh dung: tin hieu lan chinh xac tren mat cau, khong de lai "duoi". Neu $n=2$ thi co duoi song — khac hoan toan.
2

Khai niem can biet

Tat ca la moi. Nhung o co vien vang la can hoc ngay.

moi
Phuong trinh truyen song 3D
Phuong trinh $u_{tt} - c^2\Delta u = f$ mo ta cach mot tin hieu (song am, song dien tu...) lan ra xung quanh voi toc do $c$. O day $c=1$, $\Delta u = u_{xx}+u_{yy}+u_{zz}$.
moi
Bai toan Cauchy (IVP) cho PDE bac hai
Tim $u(x,t)$ cho $t>0$ khi biet trang thai ban dau: vi tri $u(\cdot,0)=g$ va van toc $u_t(\cdot,0)=h$. Mien la toan $\mathbb{R}^3$ nen khong can them dieu kien bien theo $x$.
moi
Toan tu Laplace $\Delta$ va toan tu d'Alembert $\Box$
$\Delta u = \partial_{xx}u + \partial_{yy}u + \partial_{zz}u$ la phan "khong gian" cua phuong trinh. $\Box = \partial_{tt} - c^2\Delta$ la toan tu song. Symbol $-\tau^2+c^2|\xi|^2$ doi dau → loai hyperbolic.
moi
Phan loai PDE: elliptic / parabolic / hyperbolic
Nhin vao dang toan phuong cac dao ham bac cao nhat: Laplace ($\Delta$) la elliptic, phuong trinh nhiet ($\partial_t - \Delta$) la parabolic, phuong trinh song ($\partial_{tt} - \Delta$) la hyperbolic.
moi
Ham dac trung (indicator) $\chi_A$
$\chi_A(x) = 1$ neu $x \in A$, bang $0$ neu ngoai $A$. Dung de viet nguon va du lieu mot cach gon: $f = \chi_{[0,1]}(t)\cdot\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$ co nghia la $f=1$ khi $0\le t\le 1$ va $-1\le x\le 1$, bang $0$ o chon khac.
moi
Van toc truyen huu han / non song anh sang
Nhieu loan lan voi toc do khong qua $c=1$. Gia su nguon o goc toa do, thi diem cach xa hon $t$ (theo don vi khong gian phu hop) chua the "cam" tin hieu truoc thoi diem $t$. Day la ly do bai hay hoi: "tai diem $x=(100,y,0)$, khi nao $u=0$?"
moi
Mien anh huong va mien phu thuoc
Nghiem tai $(x_0,t_0)$ chi bi tac dong boi du lieu trong qua cau $|y-x_0|\le t_0$ (neu $c=1$) va boi nguon trong non lui $|y-x_0|\le t_0-s$. Neu gia cua $g$, $h$, $f$ hoan toan nam ngoai vung do thi $u(x_0,t_0)=0$.
moi
Nguyen ly Huygens manh (chi dung $n=3$ so chieu le)
Trong $\mathbb{R}^3$, nghiem tai $(x,t)$ chi phu thuoc du lieu tren mat cau $|y-x|=t$, khong phai ca qua cau be trong. Tin hieu la "xung": den roi di, khong de lai duoi. Trong $\mathbb{R}^2$ thi nguoc lai — co "duoi" song, tin hieu tat hon rat cham.
moi
Nghiem yeu / nghiem phan bo
Du lieu la ham dac trung — gian doan tai bien cua cac tap. Nghiem $u$ khong the la ham tron $C^2$. Thay vao do, $u$ duoc hieu la nghiem theo nghia yeu: thoa man PDE sau khi nhan voi ham kiem thu tron roi tich phan.
3

Phuong phap giai va dieu kien ap dung

Thu tu thong thuong: Kirchhoff → Duhamel → Chong cat → Phan tich mien.

moi Cong thuc Kirchhoff — giai phan thuan nhat ($f=0$)

Voi $u_{tt} = \Delta u$, $u(\cdot,0)=g$, $u_t(\cdot,0)=h$ trong $\mathbb{R}^3$:

$$u(x,t) = \partial_t\!\left(t\,\fint_{\partial B_t(x)} g\,dS\right) + t\,\fint_{\partial B_t(x)} h\,dS.$$
Dieu kien ap dung Phuong trinh song hyperbolic, so chieu $n=3$, thuan nhat (khong co $f$), he so hang. Du lieu $g\in C^3$, $h\in C^2$ cho nghiem cu dien. Neu du lieu gian doan (nhu $h=\chi_{B_1}$) thi chi co nghiem yeu nhung van dung duoc cong thuc.
Nho $\fint_{\partial B_r(x)}$ la trung binh tren mat cau ban kinh $r$. Nghiem tai $(x,t)$ chi "nhin" du lieu tren mat cau ban kinh $t$ — bieu hien truc tiep cua Huygens manh.
moi Nguyen ly Duhamel — xu ly nguon $f$

Voi $u_{tt}-\Delta u=f$, $u(\cdot,0)=u_t(\cdot,0)=0$:

$$u(x,t) = \int_0^t w(x,t;\,s)\,ds,$$

trong do $w(\cdot,\cdot;\,s)$ la nghiem bai toan thuan nhat: $w_{tt}=\Delta w$, $w(x,s;s)=0$, $w_t(x,s;s)=f(x,s)$.

Dieu kien ap dung PDE tuyen tinh tien hoa. Nguon $f$ kha tich dia phuong. Sau do cong voi nghiem thuan nhat de co nghiem day du.
Meo Duhamel bien mot bai co nguon thanh vo so bai khong nguon nho hon. Moi gia tri $f(\cdot,s)$ dong vai "van toc ban dau" cua bai toan con tai thoi diem $s$.
moi Nguyen ly chong cat (superposition) — tach bai toan

PDE tuyen tinh → viet $u = u^{(h)} + u^{(f)}$ roi giai rieng. Vi $h = \chi_{B_1(0,0,2)} - \chi_{B_1(0,0,-2)}$ la hieu hai ham, tuyen tinh cho phep tach tiep thanh hai bai toan con roi lay hieu ket qua.

Dieu kien ap dung Chi dung khi PDE va dieu kien ban dau deu tuyen tinh theo $u$. PDE phi tuyen thi khong ap dung duoc.
moi Phan tich mien phu thuoc — ket luan $u=0$ tai diem xa

Tinh khoang cach tu diem quan sat $(x_0,t_0)$ den gia cua $h$ va $f$. Neu gia hoan toan nam ngoai non lui (khong cat mien phu thuoc) thi $u(x_0,t_0) = 0$. Trong 3D ket hop Huygens manh: chi can gia cua $h$ khong cat mat cau $|y-x_0|=t_0$.

Dieu kien ap dung PDE hyperbolic, toc do huu han ($c=1$). Biet gia compact cua $g$, $h$, $f$.
Meo Neu $x_0 = (100,y,0)$ thi $|x_0 - (0,0,\pm 2)| \approx 100$. Diem xa nhu vay chi nhan tin hieu sau thoi gian $t\approx 100$. Truoc do $u=0$.
moi Phuong phap ha thap (method of descent)

Nguon $\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$ chi phu thuoc bien $x$, doc lap voi $y,z$. Co the quy bai toan ve so chieu thap hon, hoac tinh tich phan theo $y,z$ trong cong thuc Kirchhoff.

Dieu kien ap dung Du lieu hoac nguon doc lap voi mot so bien khong gian.
4

Dang bai lan can

Vien tim = lan can manh (cung loai bai, xuat hien ca 3 lang kinh: khai niem + phuong phap + loai PDE). Nen hoc nhung bai vien tim truoc.

Cauchy song 3D thuan nhat — cong thuc Kirchhoff
Phuong phap: Cong thuc Kirchhoff (trung binh mat cau)
Day chinh la bai nay khi bo nguon $f=0$. Phai nam chac cong thuc nay truoc khi dung Duhamel.
Cauchy song 3D khong thuan nhat — Duhamel
Phuong phap: Nguyen ly Duhamel + Kirchhoff
Chinh xac la bai nay. Can hieu Duhamel de xu ly nguon $\chi_{[0,1]}(t)\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$ va cong vao nghiem thuan nhat.
Cauchy song 1D — cong thuc d'Alembert
Phuong phap: Cong thuc d'Alembert
Truong hop don gian nhat. Nguon $\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$ doc lap voi $y,z$ goi tinh 1D. Hoc de hieu du lieu ban dau, van toc huu han, mien phu thuoc.
Cauchy song 2D — cong thuc Poisson, phuong phap ha thap
Phuong phap: Cong thuc Poisson / method of descent
So chieu chan $n=2$ → khong co Huygens manh, co duoi song. Hoc de hieu tai sao $n=3$ dac biet.
Song tu du lieu gia compact / doi xung cau
Phuong phap: Doi bien $v=ru$, phan tich mat cau Kirchhoff
Du lieu $\chi_{B_1(0,0,\pm 2)}$ la ham dac trung hinh cau. Ky thuat $v=ru$ quy bai 3D doi xung cau ve bai 1D, rat hay xuat hien trong bai tap.
Danh gia nghiem tai diem xa nguon
Phuong phap: Hinh hoc non dac trung / van toc huu han
Bai thi hay hoi: "khi nao $u(100,y,0,t)=0$, khi nao khac 0?" — can tinh khoang cach tu $(100,y,0)$ den gia cua $h$ va $f$.
Song voi nguon co gia compact theo thoi gian va khong gian
Phuong phap: Duhamel + phan tich gia theo non
Nguon tach bien $f=\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)\chi_{[0,1]}(t)$ la nguon co gia huu han. Ky nang chinh: phan tich xem non lan truyen co cat gia cua nguon khong.
Song voi du lieu gian doan — nghiem yeu
Phuong phap: Nghiem phan bo / cong thuc tich phan
Lien quan khai niem: du lieu gian doan buoc dung nghia yeu. Khong phai focus chinh cua bai.
Cauchy phuong trinh nhiet 3D
Phuong phap: Nhan chap voi nhan nhiet (heat kernel)
Cung la PDE tien hoa tren $\mathbb{R}^3$ nhung loai parabolic (bac 1 theo $t$). Hoc de phan biet voi song.
Phuong trinh Laplace / Poisson trong $\mathbb{R}^3$
Phuong phap: Ham Green / nghiem co ban Newton
Co cung $\Delta$ nhung la loai elliptic, khong co bien $t$. Hoc de hieu su khac biet elliptic vs hyperbolic.
5

Ly thuyet chi tiet cho cac khai niem moi

Cac dinh nghia day du, co cong thuc, de tra cuu.

Phan loai PDE bac hai

Xet PDE bac hai tuyen tinh $\sum_{i,j} a_{ij}\partial_{x_i}\partial_{x_j}u + \text{(bac thap)} = f$. Phan loai dua vao dang toan phuong phan chinh $\sum a_{ij}\xi_i\xi_j$:

  • Elliptic: xac dinh dau, vi du Laplace $\Delta$, symbol $|\xi|^2 > 0$.
  • Parabolic: suy bien mot huong, vi du phuong trinh nhiet $\partial_t - \Delta$.
  • Hyperbolic: doi dau, chi so quan tinh $(1,n)$, vi du song $\partial_{tt}-\Delta$, symbol $-\tau^2+|\xi|^2$.

Phuong trinh song nay: coi $(t,x)\in\mathbb{R}^{1+3}$, symbol chinh $-\tau^2+|\xi|^2$ mang metric Lorentz $\mathrm{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$ → hyperbolic.

Bai toan Cauchy cho phuong trinh song
$$\begin{cases} u_{tt} - c^2\Delta u = f(x,t), & x\in\mathbb{R}^3,\ t>0,\\ u(x,0) = g(x),\\ u_t(x,0) = h(x). \end{cases}$$

O de bai: $c=1$, $g\equiv 0$, $h = \chi_{B_1(0,0,2)} - \chi_{B_1(0,0,-2)}$, $f = \chi_{[0,1]}(t)\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$.

Mat $t=0$ la mat khong dac trung doi voi toan tu song → bai toan dat chinh (well-posed theo Hadamard): ton tai nghiem duy nhat, phu thuoc lien tuc vao du lieu.

Cong thuc Kirchhoff (3D, thuan nhat)

Nghiem cua $u_{tt} = \Delta u$, $u(\cdot,0)=g$, $u_t(\cdot,0)=h$ trong $\mathbb{R}^3$ ($c=1$):

$$u(x,t) = \partial_t\!\left(t\,\fint_{\partial B_t(x)} g\,dS\right) + t\,\fint_{\partial B_t(x)} h\,dS,$$

trong do $\fint_{\partial B_r(x)} = \dfrac{1}{4\pi r^2}\int_{\partial B_r(x)}$ la trung binh mat cau ban kinh $r$ tam $x$.

Nho Nghiem tai $(x,t)$ chi phu thuoc du lieu tren mat cau $|y-x|=t$. Day la nguyen ly Huygens manh du' trong cong thuc.
Nguyen ly Duhamel

Voi $u_{tt}-\Delta u = f$, $u(\cdot,0)=u_t(\cdot,0)=0$:

$$u(x,t) = \int_0^t w(x,t;\,s)\,ds,$$

trong do $w(\cdot,\cdot;\,s)$ giai bai toan thuan nhat $w_{tt}=\Delta w$ voi $w(x,s;s)=0$, $w_t(x,s;s)=f(x,s)$.

Noi don gian: moi "luc nguon phat" tai thoi diem $s$ tao ra mot song rieng, lan truyen tu $s$ tro di. Nghiem tong la tich phan tat ca cac song con do.

Nguyen ly Huygens manh ($\mathbb{R}^3$)

Trong $\mathbb{R}^n$ voi $n$ le, $n\ge 3$ (dac biet $n=3$):

Nghiem tai $(x,t)$ chi phu thuoc du lieu ban dau tren mat cau $\{y: |y-x|=ct\}$, khong phai trong toan qua cau $|y-x|\le ct$.

Hau qua thuc te: mot nguon phat ngan gon (gia compact) tao ra tin hieu co "mat truoc" va "mat sau" ro rang, din hieu den roi di, khong de lai duoi.

Trong $\mathbb{R}^2$ ($n$ chan): nguyen ly Huygens sai. Tin hieu den roi chua het ngay — co "duoi song".

Dieu kien $n\ge 3$ le. He so hang. Phan thuan nhat. (Phan nguon qua Duhamel co the co hieu ung duoi.)
Van toc truyen huu han va mien phu thuoc

Tinh chat co ban cua PDE hyperbolic (c=1):

  • Neu gia cua du lieu trong $K$ thi $u(x,t)=0$ voi moi $x$ co $\mathrm{dist}(x,K) > t$.
  • Mien phu thuoc cua $(x_0,t_0)$: du lieu trong $\overline{B_{t_0}(x_0)}$ (3D: tren mat cau $\partial B_{t_0}$) va nguon trong non lui $\{|y-x_0|\le t_0-s,\, 0\le s\le t_0\}$.
  • Mien anh huong cua du lieu tai $x_0$: non tien $\{(x,t): |x-x_0|\le t\}$.
$$u(x_0,t_0) = 0 \quad \text{khi gia cua } g,h,f \text{ khong cat mien phu thuoc cua } (x_0,t_0).$$
Ham dac trung va nghiem yeu
$$\chi_A(x) = \begin{cases}1 & x\in A \\ 0 & x\notin A\end{cases}$$

Du lieu $h = \chi_{B_1(0,0,2)} - \chi_{B_1(0,0,-2)}$ gian doan tai bien hinh cau. Nguon $f$ gian doan tai bien hinh hop. Nghiem $u$ khong co dien $C^2$.

Nghiem yeu: $u$ thoa man PDE theo nghia tich phan — voi moi $\varphi\in C_c^\infty$ (ham kiem thu): $$\iint \bigl(u\,\varphi_{tt} - u\,\Delta\varphi\bigr)\,dx\,dt = \iint f\,\varphi\,dx\,dt + \text{(du lieu ban dau)}.$$

Nho Trong thuc te, van dung cong thuc Kirchhoff va Duhamel binh thuong. Chi can biet ket qua la nghiem yeu, khong doi $u$ phai tron.
