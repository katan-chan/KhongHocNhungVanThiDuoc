\textbf{Toán tử Laplace theo tọa độ.}
$$\text{Cực (2D): }\ \Delta u=u_{rr}+\tfrac1r u_r+\tfrac{1}{r^2}u_{\theta\theta}.$$
$$\text{Trụ: }\ \Delta u=u_{zz}+u_{rr}+\tfrac1r u_r+\tfrac{1}{r^2}u_{\theta\theta}.$$
$$\text{Cầu (đối xứng, theo }r\text{): }\ \Delta u=u_{rr}+\tfrac2r u_r=\tfrac1r (r u)_{rr}.$$
