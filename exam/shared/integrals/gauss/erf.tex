% integral: erf
\textbf{Hàm sai số (error function).}
$$\operatorname{erf}(x)=\frac{2}{\sqrt{\pi}}\int_0^{x} e^{-s^2}\,ds,\qquad
\operatorname{erfc}(x)=1-\operatorname{erf}(x),\qquad \operatorname{erf}(\infty)=1.$$
Tích phân Gauss hữu hạn: $\displaystyle\int_a^b e^{-s^2}\,ds=\tfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}\big(\operatorname{erf}(b)-\operatorname{erf}(a)\big).$
