% integral: moment-gauss
\textbf{Moment của Gauss ($a>0$).}
$$\int_0^{\infty} e^{-ax^2}\,dx=\tfrac12\sqrt{\tfrac{\pi}{a}},\quad
\int_0^{\infty} x\,e^{-ax^2}\,dx=\tfrac{1}{2a},\quad
\int_0^{\infty} x^2 e^{-ax^2}\,dx=\tfrac{1}{4}\sqrt{\tfrac{\pi}{a^3}}.$$
Tổng quát qua Gamma: $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty x^{k}e^{-ax^2}\,dx=\tfrac{1}{2}a^{-(k+1)/2}\,\Gamma\!\big(\tfrac{k+1}{2}\big).$
