# Toan tu Laplace

## Dinh nghia

Toan tu $\Delta u=\sum_i \partial^2 u/\partial x_i^2$; phan khong gian cua phuong trinh song, quyet dinh tinh dang huong cua lan truyen.

Trong $\mathbb{R}^3$: $\Delta u = \partial_{xx}u + \partial_{yy}u + \partial_{zz}u$.

Xuat hien trong phuong trinh song nhu phan khong gian cua toan tu d'Alembert: $\Box = \partial_t^2 - c^2\Delta$.
