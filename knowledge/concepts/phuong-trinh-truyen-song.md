# Phuong trinh truyen song (3D)

## Dinh nghia

PDE bac hai hyperbolic $u_{tt}-c^2\Delta u=f$ mo ta lan truyen nhieu loan voi van toc huu han; o day $c=1$, $n=3$, $\Delta=\partial_{xx}+\partial_{yy}+\partial_{zz}$.

Bai toan Cauchy day du:
$$\begin{cases} u_{tt}-c^2\Delta u = f(x,t), & x\in\mathbb{R}^n,\ t>0,\\ u(x,0)=u_0(x),\\ u_t(x,0)=u_1(x). \end{cases}$$

O de bai $c=1$, $n=3$, $\Delta u=u_{xx}+u_{yy}+u_{zz}$, $u_0\equiv 0$, $u_1=h$, $f=\chi_{[0,1]}(t)\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$. Day la PDE bac hai, tuyen tinh, he so hang, khong thuan nhat, loai hyperbolic.
