# Bai toan Neumann (BVP)

## Dinh nghia

Nhu bai toan Dirichlet nhung cho **dao ham phap tuyen tren bien** thay vi gia tri an:
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial n}\Big|_{\partial\Omega}=\psi.$$
Nghiem xac dinh sai khac mot hang so cong.
