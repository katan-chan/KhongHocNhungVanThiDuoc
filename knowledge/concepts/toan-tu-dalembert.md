# Toan tu d'Alembert $\Box=\partial_t^2-c^2\Delta$

## Dinh nghia

Toan tu d'Alembert $\Box \equiv \partial_t^2 - c^2\Delta = \partial_t^2 - c^2\sum_{i=1}^{n}\partial_{x_i}^2$.

Symbol toan tu $p(\tau,\xi) = -\tau^2 + c^2|\xi|^2$.

Vi $p$ doi dau (khong xac dinh), $\Box$ la toan tu hyperbolic; mat dac trung la non anh sang $\tau^2 = c^2|\xi|^2$.

Symbol chinh $-\tau^2+c^2|\xi|^2$ co metric Lorentz $\mathrm{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$ -> tinh hyperbolic, mat dac trung la non anh sang.
