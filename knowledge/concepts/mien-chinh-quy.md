# Mien chinh quy & ham can (barrier)

## Dinh nghia
Diem bien x0 chinh quy neu co ham can (dieu hoa gan x0, duong tru tai x0). Dieu kien hinh cau ngoai => chinh quy. Mien chinh quy => bai Dirichlet luon giai duoc => ham Green ton tai & duy nhat.
