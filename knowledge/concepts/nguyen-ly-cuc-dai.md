# Nguyen ly cuc dai (elliptic)

## Dinh nghia
Ham dieu hoa (Delta u = 0) dat cuc dai va cuc tieu tren BIEN mien. He qua: m <= u <= M tren bien => m <= u <= M trong mien (NLSS); nghiem Dirichlet duy nhat.
