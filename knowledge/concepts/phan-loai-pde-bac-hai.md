# Phan loai PDE bac hai elliptic/parabolic/hyperbolic

## Dinh nghia

Voi PDE bac hai tuyen tinh $\sum_{i,j} a_{ij}\partial_{x_i}\partial_{x_j}u + (\text{bac thap}) = f$, phan loai theo dang toan phuong phan chinh $\sum a_{ij}\xi_i\xi_j$:

- **Elliptic**: xac dinh dau (vi du Laplace $\Delta$, symbol $|\xi|^2$)
- **Parabolic**: suy bien mot huong (phuong trinh nhiet $\partial_t-\Delta$)
- **Hyperbolic**: dang toan phuong khong xac dinh dau voi chi so quan tinh $(1,n)$

Voi phuong trinh song $u_{tt}-\Delta u$, coi $(t,x)\in\mathbb{R}^{1+3}$, symbol chinh la $-\tau^2+|\xi|^2$ co metric Lorentz $\mathrm{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$ -> hyperbolic.
