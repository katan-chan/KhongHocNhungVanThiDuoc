# Bai toan Cauchy (IVP) cho PDE bac hai theo thoi gian

## Dinh nghia

Bai toan Cauchy (IVP) cho phuong trinh song: tim $u$ thoa
$$\begin{cases} u_{tt}-\Delta u=f, & x\in\mathbb{R}^3,\ t>0,\\ u(x,0)=g(x),\\ u_t(x,0)=h(x). \end{cases}$$

Vi mat $t=0$ la mat khong dac trung doi voi toan tu song, theo dinh ly Cauchy-Kovalevskaya / ly thuyet song, bai toan dat chinh (well-posed theo Hadamard) tren $\mathbb{R}^n$ khong can dieu kien bien khong gian.

Tim nghiem PDE tien hoa bac hai theo $t$ tren toan khong gian $\mathbb{R}^n$ khi biet $u(\cdot,0)=g$ va $u_t(\cdot,0)=h$; khong co dieu kien bien khong gian.
