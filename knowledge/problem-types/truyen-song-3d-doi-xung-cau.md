# Truyen song 3D co nguon - doi xung cau (v=ru)

## Phuong phap
- Doi bien v = r u -> song 1D: v_tt = c^2 v_rr, v(0,t)=0.
- Giai d'Alembert (thac trien le qua r=0), roi u = v/r.
- Tam r=0: u(0,t) = v_r(0,t).
