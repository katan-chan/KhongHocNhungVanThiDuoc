# Phuong phap ha thap / giam chieu (method of descent)

## Dieu kien ap dung

Du lieu/nguon khong phu thuoc mot hoac nhieu bien khong gian (vd nguon $\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$ doc lap voi $y,z$); quy bai toan 3D ve bai toan hieu dung so chieu thap hon, hoac suy nghiem 1D/2D tu cong thuc 3D.

Nguyen tac: neu $u_0(x)=u_0(x_1)$ chi phu thuoc mot bien, bai toan 3D quy ve 1D; tuong tu voi 2 bien -> 2D. Cong thuc Poisson (2D) suy ra tu Kirchhoff (3D) qua phuong phap nay.
