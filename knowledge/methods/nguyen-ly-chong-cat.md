# Nguyen ly chong cat (tuyen tinh)

## Dieu kien ap dung

PDE tuyen tinh: tach nghiem thanh tong phan do $g$, phan do $h$, va phan do nguon $f$; cung tach du lieu thanh hieu hai ham dac trung $\chi_{B_1(0,0,2)}-\chi_{B_1(0,0,-2)}$ thanh hai bai toan con.

Ap dung duoc khi phuong trinh va dieu kien ban dau la tuyen tinh theo $u$: neu $u_1$ giai bai toan voi $(g_1, h_1, f_1)$ va $u_2$ giai voi $(g_2, h_2, f_2)$ thi $\alpha u_1 + \beta u_2$ giai voi $(\alpha g_1+\beta g_2, \alpha h_1+\beta h_2, \alpha f_1+\beta f_2)$.
