# Nguyen ly Duhamel (xu ly so hang nguon)

## Dieu kien ap dung

Phuong trinh tien hoa tuyen tinh khong thuan nhat $u_{tt}-c^2\Delta u=f$; chuyen nguon $f$ thanh tich phan theo $s$ cua nghiem thuan nhat voi van toc ban dau $f(\cdot,s)$. Yeu cau $f$ kha tich dia phuong; ket hop chong cat voi nghiem thuan nhat.

Cong thuc:
$$u(x,t)=\int_0^t w(x,t;s)\,ds$$

trong do $w(x,t;s)$ giai $w_{tt}=c^2\Delta w$ voi $w(x,s;s)=0$, $w_t(x,s;s)=f(x,s)$.
